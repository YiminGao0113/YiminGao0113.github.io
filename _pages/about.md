I am a PhD student in [Electrical and Computer Engineering](https://engineering.virginia.edu/departments/electrical-and-computer-engineering) at the [University of Virginia](https://www.virginia.edu/), advised by [Prof. Mircea R. Stan](https://engineering.virginia.edu/faculty/mircea-r-stan).

My research focuses on hardware–algorithm co-design for efficient machine learning systems. I work on low-precision numerical formats, AI accelerator architecture, and efficient inference of large language models. My goal is to bridge machine learning algorithms with practical hardware implementations for energy-efficient AI systems.

Research interests
======
- AI accelerator architecture
- Hardware-aware quantization
- Hardware–software co-design
- Hardware security
